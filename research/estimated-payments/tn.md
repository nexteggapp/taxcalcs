## 1. Does Tennessee tax individual income for tax year 2026?

**No.** The Hall income tax reached interest and dividends only. It never covered wages. It is repealed for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2021.

- Source 1: "The Hall Income tax was repealed for tax periods that begin on January 1, 2021, or later."
- Source 1: "Please do not file a return for any tax year that begins on or after January 1, 2021."
- Source 2: "it does not apply to salaries, wages, and commissions."

**Wages and other earned income are constitutionally barred.** This applies to both state and local taxes. The amendment was ratified on November 4, 2014. It is in the current constitution. (corrected in verification)

- Source 3 (amendment text): "the Legislature shall not levy, authorize or otherwise permit any state or local tax upon payroll or earned personal income"
- Source 5 (current Art. II, Sec. 28): the same sentence appears, ending "nothing contained herein shall be construed as prohibiting any tax in effect on January 1, 2011"
- Source 6 (2014 results): Constitutional Amendment 3 received 882,926 yes and 450,522 no votes statewide

**Capital gains: no individual tax found.**

## 2. Estimated payment rules

Not applicable. No individual income-type tax applies in 2026. Hall income tax estimated payments ended with the repeal.

## 3. Local income taxes

None. The constitutional amendment text in Source 3 bars any "state or local tax upon payroll or earned personal income". Hall tax revenue was shared with localities, and that tax is repealed (Source 1).

## 4. Ambiguities

- **Ratification of the 2014 amendment: confirmed in verification (corrected in verification).** The Blue Book (Source 5) prints the amendment sentence in the current Art. II, Sec. 28. The certified 2014 results (Source 6) show it passed both tests that Source 4 sets out:
  - Yes votes exceeded no votes.
  - Yes votes exceeded half of all votes for governor. Governor votes summed to 1,353,728, so the threshold was 676,864.
- **The amendment covers only "earned" income.** The amendment bars taxes on payroll and earned personal income. Its grandfather clause keeps "any tax in effect on January 1, 2011", which would have been the Hall tax. A future tax on unearned income (interest, dividends, gains) is not barred by that sentence. The original Article II, Section 28 power to tax income from stocks and bonds is described in SJR 1's recitals. No such tax exists for 2026.
- **November 2026 ballot.** Amendment #2 on the November 2026 ballot would bar a state property tax (Source 4). It does not concern income tax.
- **Business entity taxes.** Tennessee's franchise and excise taxes on entities were not researched. Whether they reach single-member LLCs owned by individuals is not covered here.
