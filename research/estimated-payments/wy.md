## 1. Does Wyoming tax individual income for tax year 2026?

**No individual income tax was found.**

- Source 1: Title 39, Chapter 7, "Income Taxes", contains only W.S. 39-7-101, marked "Repealed By Laws 1998, ch. 5, § 4."
- Source 2: "Unlike other states, Wyoming does not possess an individual or corporate income tax."
- Source 3: the Department of Revenue pages were fetched and contain no income tax and no statement about one.

**A statement from the Department of Revenue, or a constitutional or statutory provision saying Wyoming has no individual income tax: NOT FOUND.** Sources checked: the Department of Revenue home and Divisions pages (Source 3), Title 39 (Source 1), and a search of wyo.gov and wyoleg.gov.

The Wyoming Constitution was obtained in verification as Title 97 (Source 4). It contains no prohibition on an income tax. Art. 15, Sec. 18 assumes one could be imposed, and requires a credit for Wyoming sales, use and property taxes paid. (corrected in verification)

- Source 4, Art. 15, Sec. 18: "No tax shall be imposed upon income without allowing full credit against such tax liability for all sales, use, and ad valorem taxes paid" The finding rests on Title 39 containing no individual income tax, plus the state portal statement in Source 2. Source 2 is not a revenue department source.

## 2. Estimated payment rules

Not applicable. No individual income-type tax exists.

## 3. Local income taxes

None. The state preempts all local income and earnings taxes.

- Source 1, W.S. 39-12-101: "no county, city, town or other political subdivision shall have the right to impose, levy or collect such taxes."
- The same section preempts "income taxes, earning taxes, or any other form of tax based on wages or other income".

## 4. Ambiguities

- **Weaker sourcing than the other states.** The state-level finding rests on the absence of any tax in statute plus a state-portal statement. No Department of Revenue page or constitutional text says it directly.
- **What the repealed chapter covered.** W.S. 39-7-101, the repealed "Income Taxes" chapter, was not traced to what it had imposed before 1998.
