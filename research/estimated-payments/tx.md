## 1. Does Texas tax individual income for tax year 2026?

**No.** The constitution bars a tax on individuals' net income, including a partner's share of partnership income. No individual income tax exists on wages, interest, dividends, capital gains or anything else.

- Source 1, proposed Sec. 24-a: "The legislature may not impose a tax on the net incomes of individuals, including an individual's share of partnership and unincorporated association income."
- Source 2: the voters adopted "PROPOSITION No. 4, as submitted by House Joint Resolution No. 38, prohibiting the imposition of an individual income tax"

**Franchise tax does not reach a sole proprietor who has not formed a limited-liability entity.**

- Source 3: "A sole proprietorship that is not legally organized in a manner that limits its liability is not a taxable entity."
- Source 3: "A single-member limited liability company filing as a sole proprietor for federal income tax purposes is a taxable entity."

## 3. Local income taxes

NOT FOUND. No primary source was found that either authorises or expressly prohibits a local income tax. Sec. 24-a binds "the legislature". No local individual income tax is known from any source consulted.

Added in verification: home-rule cities' taxing power comes from Art. XI, Sec. 5. They "may levy, assess and collect such taxes as may be authorized by law or by their charters" (Source 5). No statute authorising a city income tax was found. The Tax Code and Local Government Code were not searched section by section. (corrected in verification)

## 4. Ambiguities

- **The current constitution text was read in verification (corrected in verification).** Source 4 prints Sec. 24-a, "INDIVIDUAL INCOME TAX PROHIBITED", "(Added Nov. 5, 2019.)". Source 1 also repealed the old Sec. 24, which had allowed a voter-approved income tax. It also removed "natural persons" from the Art. VIII, Sec. 1(c) power to tax incomes.
- **Franchise tax on single-member LLCs.** An individual operating through a single-member LLC is subject to the franchise tax (a margin tax on the entity). Franchise tax thresholds, rates and due dates were not researched.
